# Privacy Policy

## Introduction

Welcome to **Cuisko**. We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our recipe, meal planning and grocery list service (the "App").

Cuisko is published by **S. SELLEST**, a Société par Actions Simplifiée (SAS) with a share capital of €2,000, registered at 12 Rue de la Part-Dieu, 69003 Lyon, France — RCS Lyon 922 789 771.

---

## Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following types of data:

### Personal Identification Information
Name, email address, and any other information you provide when creating an account, whether with an email and password or with your Google account.

### Food Preferences and Dietary Information
The tastes, diets, dislikes, allergies and intolerances you indicate within the App, as well as the number of people you cook for. Allergy and intolerance information is used only to keep unsuitable recipes out of your suggestions and meal plans.

### Household Information
The households you create or join, the members you invite, and the meal plans and grocery lists you share with them.

### Recipes, Photos and Imported Content
Recipes you write or import, links to web pages you import recipes from, and photos you take with your device's camera or import from your photo library.

### Voice Input
When you use voice input, your speech is transcribed by your device's speech recognition service so that the resulting text can be used in the App.

### Messages to the Cooking Assistant
The questions and messages you send to Cuisko's AI cooking assistant.

### Technical Data
IP address, device type, operating system version, app version, push notification token, and other technical identifiers used to access our service.

### Usage Data
Information about how you use the App, including the screens you visit, recipes viewed, saved and cooked, meal plans created, and grocery lists managed.

### AI Usage Records
Each time an AI feature runs for you, we record which feature it was, the model used, the amount of processing it took and what it cost. These records never contain the text of your messages, your recipes or the AI's answers.

---

## Use of Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

### Provide and Improve Services
To operate our recipe discovery, meal planning, grocery list and household sharing features, and to continuously improve them.

### Personalized Suggestions
To suggest recipes and build meal plans that match your tastes, your dietary needs and your household.

### AI Features
Recipes you import, messages you send to the cooking assistant, and the related context (such as your preferences and meal plan) are processed by third-party AI providers listed below to deliver the requested feature — for example turning a web page into a recipe, answering a cooking question, or estimating nutrition values. AI may also be used to generate illustrative images of recipes and ingredients. This content is processed solely to deliver the feature and is not used to train third-party models without your explicit consent.

### Product Analytics
To understand which features are used and how, and what each AI feature costs us to run, so we can improve the App and keep it affordable. Usage data and AI usage records are linked to your account identifier, not to your name or email address, and are never used for advertising.

### Communication
To respond to your support inquiries and send you important service-related notifications, including push notifications you have allowed on your device.

### Marketing
To send you information about Cuisko features, updates, and promotions, where you have consented to receive such communications.

### Security
To protect the App and our users against fraud, abuse, and other unlawful activities.

## Sharing Your Information

We only share your personal information with the third-party providers listed above and in the following additional circumstances:

**Household Members** — Meal plans, grocery lists and recipes you add to a household are visible to the other members of that household.

**Legal Requirements** — When disclosure is necessary to comply with applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

**Business Transfer** — In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or part of our assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you in advance of any such change.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

## Protecting Your Information

We implement technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

---

## Retention of Your Data

We retain your personal data and content for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services.

You can delete your account at any time from the App's account settings. Your account is then scheduled for deletion and permanently deleted after **30 days**; signing back in during that period cancels the deletion. You may also request deletion by contacting us at **support@cuisko.app**.

When your account is deleted, all information that identifies you — your name, email address, sign-in methods, photos, and conversations with the cooking assistant — is permanently erased. Recipes and cooking data (such as preferences and meal plans) are retained only in anonymized form, no longer linked to any person, to keep shared households working and to produce aggregate statistics. Your membership in households shared with others is removed.

## Your Rights

In accordance with the GDPR and applicable data protection laws, you have the following rights:

- **Access** — Right to access the personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectification** — Right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Deletion** — Right to request deletion of your personal data.
- **Data Portability** — Right to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Objection** — Right to object to the processing of your personal data.
- **Withdrawal of Consent** — Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at **support@cuisko.app**. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL).

## Children's Privacy

Cuisko is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

---

## Cookies and Tracking

The App does not use advertising trackers, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Technical data is collected by our infrastructure providers to deliver the App and its updates. Usage data is sent to our product analytics provider, which stores an anonymous identifier on your device until you sign in; from then on, it is linked to your account identifier. You can object to this processing by contacting us at **support@cuisko.app**. You can manage permissions such as camera, microphone, photo library and notifications through your device settings.
